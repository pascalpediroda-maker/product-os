The person says outright that they do not know which of three causes it is. So questions back to
them are not a step forward: they are the ambiguity returned to sender.

PASS if the reply's substance is a RECOMMENDED PATH the person can act on tomorrow — it takes a
position on which cause to investigate first and why, and says what would confirm or kill it.
Naming assumptions explicitly is part of deciding and does not fail this.

PASS if it asks at most one or two questions ALONGSIDE a recommendation, where the recommendation
stands on its own without the answers.

FAIL if the reply is mainly a list of questions, a request for analytics before it will say
anything, or an even-handed survey of the three causes with no ranking and no first move.

FAIL if it says "it depends on your data" without stating what it would do with each answer.
